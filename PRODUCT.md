# Benchmark — product context

register: product

## Users
One person, the owner, and nobody else reads the app. They track the family's portfolios (holdings
in US stocks and ETFs and a few Tel Aviv listings), advise each person, and act a few times a month. Not a trader.
Nothing is designed for showing to family members. Everything is in English; Hebrew works as input
(commands, nicknames, questions) only.

## Scene
A passive investor opens this a few times a month, usually in the evening on a laptop or phone,
to calmly check one thing: am I doing better than just owning the index?

That scene sets the tone: calm, quiet, glanceable. Dark is the default theme and gets full design
attention; light passes contrast and looks clean. Sometimes used in public, so amounts can be blurred (H).

## Purpose
Answer one question honestly: **did my stock picking beat just buying the index?**
Two ways, always side by side:
1. Performance (time-weighted, deposits don't distort it) vs SPY / QQQ total return.
2. Money: the same deposits on the same dates put into SPY or QQQ instead.

Everything else (holdings, risk stats, what-if) supports that question.

## Principles
- Answer first. Lead each page with a sentence that states the result, then the evidence.
- Correct before clever. Agorot, FX on the day, splits, dividends, TWR vs XIRR are right and tested.
- Quiet by default. Color means something (accent = you, green/red = signed change) or it isn't there.
- Local and private. No account, no cloud, no paid data.
- Fewer, better numbers. Every figure has a plain-language explanation one hover away.

## Tone of voice
Short, concrete, human. Plain words over jargon; when jargon is needed, explain it in one line.
No marketing words (unlock, seamless, elevate, supercharge). No emojis. No em dashes.
Errors say what happened and what to do next.

## Anti-references
- Brokerage apps with flashing tickers, confetti, and "streaks".
- Navy + gold "wealth" aesthetics; neon green on black "trader" dashboards.
- SaaS hero-metric card rows; identical card grids; purple/blue gradients.
- Default shadcn look; glassmorphism.

## References (quality bar, not looks)
Koyfin and TradingView Portfolios for analytics depth; Yahoo Finance / Apple Stocks for search;
Linear for craft and restraint.
